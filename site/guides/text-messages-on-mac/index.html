How to get text messages on your Mac

Two ways to get the texts your iPhone gets on your Mac, what to check when they don’t arrive, and what works with Android.

UGBy Uros Gazvoda · Published 3 October 2026

Your Mac can show the same text messages as your iPhone. iMessages (blue) arrive by themselves. SMS texts (green) need one switch turned on. This guide covers both.

Short answer: on your iPhone, open Settings → Apps → Messages → Text Message Forwarding and switch on your Mac. Both devices must use the same Apple Account.

Blue and green messages

What it isReaches your Mac
BlueiMessage, sent over the internetYes, once you sign in to Messages
GreenSMS, MMS or RCS, sent through your carrierOnly with one of the two ways below

Bank alerts, delivery updates and most verification codes are green. That is why they often show up on the phone but not on the Mac.

1. Sign in to Messages on your Mac

Open the Messages app. Choose Messages → Settings → iMessage and sign in with the Apple Account you use on your iPhone. Blue messages now arrive on your Mac.

2. Turn on Text Message Forwarding

This sends green messages from your iPhone to your Mac.

  1. On your iPhone, open Settings → Apps → Messages.
  2. Tap Text Message Forwarding.
  3. Switch on your Mac in the list.

If you don’t see Text Message Forwarding, turn iMessage off and on again on the iPhone, then tap Send & Receive and sign in. These steps follow Apple’s support page.

Or: use Messages in iCloud

Messages in iCloud keeps your whole message history the same on every device. It includes forwarding, so you can skip step 2.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → iCloud → Messages, then switch it on.
  • Mac: Messages → Settings → iMessage, then tick Enable Messages in iCloud.

The trade-off: your messages take up iCloud storage, and deleting a message on one device deletes it everywhere.

Messages not showing up or not syncing

Check these in order:

  • Same Apple Account. On the Mac, Messages → Settings → iMessage shows the account. It must match the iPhone.
  • The iPhone is on and online. It receives the SMS and passes it on. Wi-Fi or mobile data both work.
  • Your Mac is switched on in the forwarding list. Switch it off and on again if it already is.
  • Your phone number is ticked on the Mac. Messages → Settings → iMessage, under “You can be reached for messages at”.
  • Sync by hand. With Messages in iCloud on, click Sync Now in the same window.
  • Restart Messages. Quit the app on the Mac and open it again.

With an Android phone

The Messages app on a Mac only works with an iPhone. With Android, use Google Messages for web: open it in your browser and scan the QR code with the Google Messages app on your phone. Your texts then appear in that browser tab.

Verification codes on your Mac

Once texts reach your Mac, the codes in them do too. Safari can offer a code in some sign-in fields. In other browsers it often does not, as the autofill guide explains.

Questions

Why do some texts reach my Mac and others don’t?

Blue messages are iMessage and reach your Mac by themselves. Green messages are SMS or RCS. They go to your iPhone first, and reach your Mac only when Messages in iCloud or Text Message Forwarding is on.

Does my iPhone have to be near my Mac?

No. It has to be switched on and online, on Wi-Fi or mobile data. It does not need to be on the same network as your Mac.

Can I get text messages on a Mac without an iPhone?

You can get iMessages with only an Apple Account. SMS texts need an iPhone to receive them. With an Android phone, use Google Messages for web in your browser.

Why do verification codes not show up on my Mac?

Most codes are sent by SMS, so they need Messages in iCloud or Text Message Forwarding. Check that your Mac is switched on in the forwarding list on your iPhone.

Is it free?

Yes. Both ways are built into the iPhone and the Mac. Your carrier may still charge for the SMS itself.
